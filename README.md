### Project Description
An AI-powered smart glasses attachment that translates your partner's innocent sighs into the exact percentage of disappointment they are currently experiencing. It uses continuous audio parsing to save you from guessing whether "it's fine" actually means disaster.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Human communication is entirely too clear, and relationships lack the high-stress guesswork of a live-wire bomb defusal. People desperately need real-time data metrics on domestic dissatisfaction so they can panic more efficiently.

###The Solution (that nobody asked for)
A wearable decibel-and-tone monitor strapped to your face that overlays live, color-coded HUD warning gauges onto your field of vision whenever a heavy breath is detected. Now you can see your relationship stability drop from 98% to 12% in crisp high-definition.
